(03) Process

How a project moves.

Fixed phases, fixed checkpoints. You always know what is being made this week.

Weeks 1–2

Discover

Workshops, interviews and an audit of your brand and competitors.

Output: strategy brief
Weeks 3–5

Define

Positioning, messaging and two or three creative directions to react to.

Output: chosen direction
Weeks 6–10

Design & build

Identity system, website and assets, reviewed together every week.

Output: finished system
Week 11 +

Launch

Go-live, guidelines and handover — then support as you grow.

Output: launch kit
Principles

What we hold ourselves to.

  1. P.01

    Strategy before style.

    We don't open a design tool until we agree what the work needs to achieve.

  2. P.02

    Senior people, start to finish.

    The people in the first meeting are the people doing the work.

  3. P.03

    Built to be used.

    Every system ships with guidelines and files your team can run with.
