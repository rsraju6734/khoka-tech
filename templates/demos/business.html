Our approach

We stay through delivery, not just the recommendations.

I.

Understand

Interviews, data review and site visits. We agree the questions that matter before proposing answers.

2–3 weeks
II.

Decide

Options with costs, risks and trade-offs laid out plainly, so leadership can choose with confidence.

1–2 weeks
III.

Implement

We work inside your team on the changes — plans, hiring, systems — with weekly progress reviews.

6–16 weeks
IV.

Hand over

Documentation, training and measures your team owns. We check in at 90 days.
