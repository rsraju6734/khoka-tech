    ( 04 ) — The studio

    Mass, light & time

    Monolith is an architecture and construction studio of 34 architects, interior designers, engineers and builders. We work on few projects at a time, and we follow each one from the first sketch to the handover of keys.

    Our philosophy is simple: use honest materials, let daylight do the decorating, and build things that will look better in thirty years than on the day they are finished.

    • i.
      Honest materials

      Concrete, stone, timber and lime — left visible, never disguised.

    • ii.
      Light as structure

      Openings are placed by the path of the sun, not by the facade grid.

    • iii.
      Built for a century

      Detailing for monsoon, heat and maintenance from day one.
