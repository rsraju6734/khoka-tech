About Nexora

Senior engineers, small teams, clear communication

Nexora was started by engineers who were tired of hand-offs. Every project has a lead engineer you can talk to directly, weekly demos of working software, and honest estimates.

  • OwnershipWe stay accountable from planning to production.
  • TransparencyShared boards, weekly demos and written decisions.
  • QualityCode reviews, automated tests and documentation by default.
