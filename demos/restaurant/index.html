Welcome

A neighbourhood restaurant with a serious kitchen.

The Urban Plate opened as a small grill on Gulshan Avenue. Today we seat 80 guests across our dining room and bar — but the idea is the same: good ingredients, a hot fire, and food you will want to share.

  • Wood-fired grillBurgers, steaks and vegetables cooked over seasoned hardwood.
  • Made in-houseFresh pasta, dough, sauces and desserts prepared every morning.
  • Market produceVegetables and herbs bought daily from local growers.
