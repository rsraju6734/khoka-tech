FAQ

Questions before you enrol

Can't find an answer? Send an enquiry from the enrolment form and our admissions team will call you.

Do I need any experience?

No. Beginner courses start from zero. Intermediate courses list what you should know on the course page.

What if I miss a live class?

Every class is recorded and available in your dashboard within a few hours.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Most courses can be paid in two or three monthly instalments.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, after completing the assignments and the final project.
