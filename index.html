Real Estate & Construction · Architecture studio

Monolith Architecture

A cinematic website for a residential architecture and construction studio. It opens with a technical blueprint that draws itself and turns into the real photograph, then walks visitors through a house — from the street to the garden pool — as they scroll.

What's inside

  • Animated blueprint intro that reveals the hero photograph (skippable, shown once per visit)
  • Floating pill navigation with active-section indicator and full-screen mobile menu
  • Pinned scroll storytelling through seven spaces with clip-path transitions and parallax
  • Filterable projects with a detail dialog
  • Services accordion with image preview, studio story, principles and animated figures
  • Project inquiry form in clearly labelled demo mode
Technology
HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, GSAP + ScrollTrigger (bundled locally) · GitHub Pages ready
Design
Instrument Serif · Manrope · JetBrains Mono · ink black, navy & blueprint blue
Responsive
Desktop, tablet and mobile · respects reduced-motion settings
